2. NANG CAP VA TOI UU MACRO DIEN HEADTEXT (SAP_FillHeadTextOnly):
   - Kiem tra dieu kien tien quyet cua du lieu truoc khi gui lenh sang SAP.
   - Tu dong lay ten user SAP (ses.Info.User) de ghi nhan lich su.
   - Phan loai chinh xac cac tinh huong:
       + HEADTEXT_DONE: Dien moi thanh cong va luu tren SAP MIGO.
       + HEADTEXT_OK: HeadText tren SAP da trung khop voi INPUT, bo qua khong ghi de.
       + HEADTEXT_EXISTS_DIFFERENT: Canh bao HeadText tren SAP khac voi So phieu INPUT.
       + HEADTEXT_MULTI_SOPHIEU / NEED_SOPHIEU: Huong dan user bo sung hoac xu ly thu cong.
       + OPEN_DOC_FAIL / HEADTEXT_READ_FAIL: Ghi ro ma loi va huong dan khac phuc.
   - Tu dong quay lai man hinh chinh MIGO sau moi DOC de luon san sang cho DOC tiep theo.
   - Hop thoai tong ket truc quan: bao cao so luong da dien moi, da khop, khac biet, va loi.
